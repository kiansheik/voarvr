Follow GUID-preserving Unity asset workflows. Setup-created settings live under `Assets/Settings/` / `Assets/XR/` after import; they are shared sources, not ignored caches. Never search/edit generated Library blobs to change first-party behavior.

Endless-world ownership and budgets: [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md). Reproducible evidence lives under ignored `artifacts/reviews/infinite-world-landing-v1/`; WorldReview exposes explicit glide, actual traversal, contact and camera captures. Scene changes use Unity editor serialization.

Flight instruments: `UI/FlightHud.cs` and `World/BirdAirflowTrails.cs`, installed by BirdFlightDriver after essential rig wiring. Explicit `Editor/FlightInstrumentReview.cs` captures HUD/wake/thermal evidence; `AtmosphereLiftReview.cs` reproduces failed and successful assistance candidates. Current evidence: `artifacts/reviews/flight-instruments-v1/`.

## Magpie and local motion evidence

- `Flight/BirdMorphology.cs`, `AvianWingPresentation.cs`: optional avian morphology and coupled feather/manus/alula/tail capability; legacy Duck and membrane Dragon preserve defaults.
- `Telemetry/`: versioned scalar binary schema, bounded writer, runtime capture and simple calibrated replay. Offline `tools/scripts/telemetry.py`; operational contract `docs/development/telemetry.md`.
- `blender/scripts/create_magpie.py`, `magpie_measurements.py`: original63-bone asset and overlap-aware rest-area validation. Research provenance in `docs/research/species/magpie.md`.
- `Editor/MagpieSetup.cs`, `MagpieReview.cs`, `TelemetryBuildStamp.cs`: explicit catalog setup, actual-frame pose evidence, build identity capture. No scene rewrites or runtime MCP dependency.

## Critique and future production

- `Gameplay/{RouteHomeChapter,FlightJourneyStore,ExpeditionDirector,FlightChallenge,SkyForaging}`: untimed chapter, persistent restoration/checkpoints and bounded foraging durability; validated schema and injected storage.
- `UI/{CharacterSelectController,FlightMenu,FlightPreferences}`: preflight, deliberate paused help and independent persisted preferences. `Input/FlightActionGate` prevents held menu input entering flight. Driver coordinates actual-support recovery and saves.
- `World/{JourneyPresentation,RouteBearing}`: bounded seed/carrier/bloom, stage-specific destination markers and gold route chevrons; sampled lift and shared logical targets. Non-text offscreen bearing follows the final camera pose; actor-relative seed transfer/carry confirms possession. `FlightFeedback` owns optional cues and a spatial crown voice.
- `Editor/RouteHomeReview`: explicit isolated controller routes and labeled visual fixtures, never import-time mutations. `Tests/PlayMode/PlayModeSaveIsolation` protects real saves through editor-only adapter overrides.
- [First adventure implementation](../reviews/2026-09-10-route-home-implementation.md) and [handoff](session-handoffs/2026-09-10-route-home-implementation.md).

- [2026-09-10 game critique/roadmap](../reviews/2026-09-10-game-critique-and-roadmap.md): repository coverage, build-specific playtest chronology, provisional rubric, proposed story/session/asset direction and 24 W work orders. This is the entry point for future implementation briefs.
- Independent visual/player/technical reports sit beside it in `docs/reviews/`; ignored `artifacts/reviews/` images and `artifacts/telemetry/` recordings are local evidence, with durable observations summarized in reports.
- Current progression/scoring ownership: `FlightChallenge.Complete` keeps efficiency scoring in Training and movement rewards in RouteHome; `FlightProgress` stores local bests. `ExpeditionDirector` owns stage/rest/lifecycle checkpoints through `FlightJourneyStore`; `SkyForaging` saves bounded improvements and on lifecycle transitions. Future work must preserve/migrate these records.
