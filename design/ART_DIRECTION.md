# Art direction

Undecided: final realism/stylization, palette, species, biome, UI voice and audio identity. Do not acquire asset packs to settle these questions implicitly.

For M0 use readable primitive silhouettes, plain contrasting materials, a visible ground plane and distinct perch heights. Early Blender assets should support scale/orientation/perch experiments and run efficiently on standalone VR. Prioritize clear depth/heading cues over detail. [Asset guidelines](../docs/ASSET_GUIDELINES.md) define the initial production constraints.
