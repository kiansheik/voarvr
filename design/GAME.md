# Game vision

Feel like a bird by moving your arms: flap for effort, extend into a glide, bank around natural landmarks, dive and settle onto a perch. Quest 3 is the first target; a future gamepad presentation should share the underlying bird simulation.

The long-term loop connects exploration, food, wildlife and manageable physical exertion. Different bird sizes/species may change movement and world scale. The foundation only proves inputs, state, presentation and development tooling are connected. There is no progression, fitness scoring or ecology yet.

Prioritize responsive flight, clear spatial feedback and repeatable hardware testing before adding world size or asset detail. See [roadmap](../docs/ROADMAP.md).
